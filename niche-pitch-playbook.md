## Category 1: Appointment-Driven Businesses

**Core pain:** Revenue is 100% slot-based. Every no-show is direct revenue loss. The phone rings during treatment. Manual reminders are inconsistent. Re-booking lapsed clients takes time no one has.

### Pitch
"You're losing roughly 20% of your booked appointments to no-shows — and you probably don't know the exact number. Our system sends automated WhatsApp and SMS reminders 48 hours and 2 hours before every appointment. It answers booking calls with an AI voice agent when you're with a client. And it identifies patients you haven't seen in 6 months and reaches out automatically. Clinics we work with recover 15–18 new appointments per month from re-engagement alone. Zero effort on your side."

### Features Used
- Automated 48h + 2h reminders (WhatsApp primary, SMS fallback)
- Voice AI: answers calls, books appointments, 24/7
- Waiting list: fills cancelled slots automatically
- Re-engagement: identifies and contacts lapsed clients
- Analytics dashboard: no-show rate, occupancy, recovered revenue

## Category 2: Trade & Field Service Businesses

**Core pain:** Revenue comes from jobs — but jobs are won over the phone. Missing a call while on a roof or under a car means that job went to whoever answered. No CRM, no follow-up, no re-engagement. Customers leave reviews when angry, not when happy.

### Pitch
"How many calls did you miss last week while you were on a job? Every missed call is a job that went to your competitor. Our AI answers every call, asks the right questions — what's the problem, what's the address, when are you available — and books the job into your schedule. You get a notification. They get a confirmation via WhatsApp. When the job is done, it automatically asks for a Google review. Most tradespeople add 4–8 new jobs per month just from calls they were already missing."

### Features Used
- Voice AI: answers all incoming calls 24/7, qualifies leads, books job slots
- Job confirmation + status update via WhatsApp/SMS
- Post-job follow-up: automated Google review request
- Google review manager: AI monitors and drafts replies to all reviews

## Category 3: Hospitality & Venue Businesses

**Core pain:** Reservation no-shows leave tables and slots empty. Bad Google reviews accumulate unanswered. Cancellations create gaps that could be filled from a waiting list. High-volume repetitive communication (confirmations, reminders, offers) happens manually or not at all.

### Pitch
"An empty table on a Saturday night that had a reservation costs more than just the cover — it's the ripple effect on staff, food prep, and morale. Our system sends reservation reminders automatically so guests actually show up. It monitors every Google review and drafts a reply within minutes — positive or negative. And when a cancellation happens, it notifies the waiting list instantly to fill the gap. Hotels and restaurants using automated review management improve their Google rating by 0.4–0.8 stars within 90 days."

### Features Used
- Automated reservation reminders (WhatsApp + SMS)
- Waiting list backfill on cancellation
- Google review monitor + AI-drafted replies
- Re-engagement campaigns (seasonal offers, off-peak promotions)

## Category 4: Professional & Consulting Services

**Core pain:** Revenue comes from client relationships — not walk-ins. Leads go cold because follow-up is inconsistent. Past clients never hear from the firm again after the engagement ends. Consultation scheduling is done through email back-and-forth. The business has no client communication infrastructure.

### Pitch
"Your past clients are your cheapest source of new revenue — but most firms never contact them again after the engagement ends. Our system identifies every client who hasn't had contact in 6+ months and triggers a personalized re-engagement message. New consultation requests are handled by an AI that qualifies the inquiry and books them directly into your calendar — no email back-and-forth. Law firms and accounting offices using re-engagement automation average 3–5 new conversations per month from clients they'd written off."

### Features Used
- Consultation booking automation (no-code booking link + calendar integration)
- Automated follow-up sequences post-engagement
- Re-engagement campaigns for dormant clients
- Voice AI: handles initial inquiry calls, qualifies and routes
- Analytics: client contact gaps, conversion tracking

## Category 5: Education & Children's Services

**Core pain:** High volume of repetitive parent inquiries. Trial class and enrollment scheduling is manual. Lesson reminders are sent inconsistently — leading to no-shows and wasted slots. Seasonal enrollment periods create communication overload. Parent communication is fragmented across phone, WhatsApp, and email.

### Pitch
"Your front desk spends hours every week answering the same five questions from parents and coordinating trial class bookings one by one. Our AI handles all incoming inquiries, books trial classes automatically, and sends lesson reminders so students actually show up. During enrollment season — when 50 parents inquire in one week — the AI handles all 50 simultaneously without a single missed message. You focus on the students. The AI handles the parents."

### Features Used
- Voice AI: handles parent inquiries, books trial classes, answers FAQs
- Automated lesson/session reminders (WhatsApp + SMS)
- Re-enrollment campaigns (end of term, season start)
- Student/parent database with full contact history
- Waiting list management for popular courses

## Category 6: Retail with Consultation Appointments

**Core pain:** Revenue depends on consultation appointments that frequently go no-show — wasting staff prep time and allocated slots. Post-purchase follow-up never happens, so there is no repeat business cycle. Dormant customers are never reactivated. Positive experiences don't automatically translate into Google reviews.

### Pitch
"You spend time preparing for a consultation — pulling measurements, samples, stock — and then the customer doesn't show. Our system sends automated reminders so prepared visits actually happen. After a sale, it follows up with a satisfaction check and drives a Google review. And when a customer hasn't come back in 90 days, it sends a personalized re-engagement message — bringing back revenue you'd written off without any manual effort."

### Features Used
- Appointment confirmation + reminder automation
- Post-sale follow-up sequences (satisfaction + review request)
- Re-engagement for customers inactive 60–90+ days
- Google review automation
